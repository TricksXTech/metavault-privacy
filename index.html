MetaVault Wallet

Privacy Policy

Effective Date: June 20, 2024

Summary Key Takeaways

  • MetaVault provides a way to manage digital assets across multiple blockchains.
  • Your recovery phrases and private keys are stored on your device using industry-standard encryption.
  • Your sensitive data, including private keys and recovery phrases, never leave your device and are never sent to any server.
  • If you use the Google Drive backup feature, a copy of your wallet data is stored in your personal cloud account.
  • We do not collect, store, or have access to your personal data or funds.

1. Introduction

MetaVault ("we," "us," or "our") is a self-custodial cryptocurrency wallet application. This Privacy Policy explains how we protect your privacy. By using MetaVault, you agree to the practices described in this policy.

2. What Data We Collect and Why

MetaVault is designed to be a private-by-default application. We do not collect any personal data.

  • Wallet Addresses: Public addresses are used locally to display your balances and transaction history.
  • Transaction History: A record of your transactions is maintained locally on your device for your convenience.
  • Device Information: We do not collect device identifiers or tracking information.
  • Contact Information: If you save addresses to your local contact list, that data stays on your device.

3. Sensitive Information (Recovery Phrases and Private Keys)

Your recovery phrases (mnemonics) and private keys are the most sensitive parts of your wallet.

  • Local Storage: These are stored securely on your device and protected by the Android KeyStore.
  • Local Processing: All wallet generation and transaction signing happen entirely on your device. Your private keys and mnemonics are never transmitted to any server or third party.
  • Control: You have full and exclusive control over your funds. We cannot access, recover, or reset your wallet if you lose your keys.

4. How Your Wallet Works

MetaVault is a self-custodial wallet. This means you are your own bank. The application acts as an interface to the blockchain, allowing you to manage assets locally without any central authority or server having access to your private information.

5. Third-Party Services We Connect To

To provide necessary wallet functionality, MetaVault may connect to the following:

  • Google Drive: Used only if you choose to back up your wallet. Your data is stored in your own Google account.
  • GitHub: Used to fetch the latest list of supported tokens and blockchains.
  • CoinGecko: Used to provide real-time market prices and charts.
  • Blockchain Networks: The app connects to blockchain nodes solely to broadcast transactions and check balances.

6. Device Permissions We Request

We ask for the following permissions to provide specific features:

  • Camera: To scan QR codes for easy address entry.
  • Biometrics (Fingerprint/Face ID): To allow you to unlock your wallet quickly and securely on your device.
  • Internet & Network State: To interact with blockchain networks and fetch price data.
  • Vibrate: For haptic feedback during app use.

7. Cloud Backups

If you enable the Google Drive backup feature:

  • A backup of your wallet information is uploaded to a folder in your Google Drive named MetaVaultBackup.
  • This file is intended to help you restore your wallet if you lose your device.
  • You are responsible for the security of your Google account.

8. Data We Do NOT Collect

  • We do not collect your name, email address, or phone number.
  • We do not track your physical location or IP address.
  • We do not have access to your private keys, recovery phrases, or funds.

9. Your Rights

MetaVault respects your privacy rights:

  • Access and Deletion: All of your data is stored locally. You can delete your data at any time by deleting your wallets within the app or uninstalling the application.
  • Backup Removal: You can delete your cloud backups at any time directly through your Google Drive account.

10. Children's Privacy

MetaVault is not intended for use by children. We do not knowingly collect information from children.

11. Changes to This Policy

We may update this policy to reflect improvements in our privacy features. Changes will be posted within the application.

12. How to Contact Us

If you have questions about this privacy policy, you can reach out to us through our official GitHub repository.
